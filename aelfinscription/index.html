Names, supply, and on-chain records

aelf Inscriptions

aelf Inscriptions is a project for named inscriptions on the aelf blockchain. An inscription has a tick, a total supply, and a limit per mint. Explore what these fields mean and the terminology used in inscription records.

Learn the concepts Explore resources

Read an inscription’s definition

Tick & SEED

A tick is the inscription’s name. In the aelf inscription design, it is associated with the symbol of an NFT-tagged SEED.

Total supply & limit per mint

Total supply defines the overall supply cap. The limit per mint defines the maximum amount for one mint; it is a separate field from total supply.

Chain & identifiers

The chain, tick, related token symbols, and transaction IDs provide context for an inscription’s records. Keep these identifiers together when reading explorer data.

Understand the terminology

Deploy

Deployment defines an inscription with a tick and supply parameters. That definition describes the inscription; it does not establish a particular account’s balance.

Issue

Issuance refers to issuing the inscription’s tokens for distribution. It is distinct from defining the inscription and from an individual mint.

Mint / Inscribe

Minting or inscribing refers to obtaining an amount under the inscription’s distribution rules. The per-mint limit and recorded transaction provide context for that amount.
