From SEED to symbol

SEED & associated symbol

A SEED is an NFT associated with a token or collection name on aelf. The SEED’s own identifier and the symbol associated with it are different pieces of information.

Fungible tokens

Units of a fungible token (FT) share one symbol and are interchangeable. A token’s definition includes its decimals and total supply; issuance distributes units of that token.

NFT collections & items

A collection groups NFT items. A collection symbol such as ABC-0 identifies the collection; item symbols identify individual items within it.

Reading token information

Names and records

Keep the chain and full symbol together when reading a record. A similar name alone does not identify the same token or NFT item.

Creation and issuance

Creation defines a token or collection. Issuance allocates units or items to recipients; these terms describe separate actions.

Holders and permissions

A holder has a balance of a token or NFT. Ownership of the token definition and issuance permissions are separate roles recorded by the contract.
